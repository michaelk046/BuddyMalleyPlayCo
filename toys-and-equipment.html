Toys & Equipment

These are toys Buddy and Malley have used, plus training equipment we're considering for the future. We keep hands-on favorites separate from wishlist items.

We separate what they have personally tried from what we're simply interested in — because there's a difference.

These are toys they own and have used. Every dog's play style is different, so always supervise play and inspect toys for damage.

Buddy's Brown Bear — KONG Wild Knots

Bought for Buddy as a puppy and is still in almost perfect shape.

Buddy's own Wild Knots bear combines a soft, cuddly exterior with a knotted rope skeleton inside. A fun choice for dogs who enjoy carrying, squeaking, and wrestling with plush toys.

View on Chewy

Malley's White Bear — KONG Wild Knots

Bought for Malley as a puppy and is still in almost perfect shape.

Malley has her own Wild Knots bear, too! Its plush outside makes it easy to cuddle and carry, while the knotted rope interior adds a different texture for playful chewing and tugging.

View on Chewy

Jolly Ball

Had for a couple years now and Buddy still loves to chase it sometimes.

A big, bouncy ball made for energetic play. Its rounded shape keeps it moving, making it a great option for dogs who love chasing and pushing toys around the yard.

View on Chewy

KONG Squeaky Football

Malley's eyes light up when she hears one squeak.

Football shape meets squeaky fun. The unpredictable bounce adds a little variety to fetch, while the built-in squeaker gives dogs another reason to chase it down.

View on Chewy

KONG Squeaky Tennis Balls

Malley's eyes light up when she hears one squeak.

A familiar fetch favorite with an added squeak. These lightweight balls are easy to toss and chase, giving ball-loving dogs an extra bit of excitement during playtime.

View on Chewy

KONG Wobbler

Part puzzle, part treat dispenser. Fill the Wobbler with kibble or suitable treats and let your dog nudge and paw it to release rewards—a different kind of play from ordinary fetch.

View on Chewy

KONG Flyer

A flexible flying disc for dogs who love to run and retrieve. Its disc shape creates a different challenge from a ball and makes outdoor fetch sessions a little more interesting.

View on Chewy

KONG Wubba

Buddy spent countless hours chewing on these before finally wearing them down.

With its long fabric tails and squeaky body, the Wubba is built for grabbing, shaking, and interactive games. A playful alternative when the usual ball just won't do.

View on Chewy

KONG Mega Wubba

Buddy spent countless hours chewing on these before finally wearing them down.

The oversized member of the Wubba family brings extra-long tails and a larger body to interactive play. Especially interesting for dogs who enjoy big toys they can grab and shake.
