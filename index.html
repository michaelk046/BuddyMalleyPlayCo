Meet the Crew

Every adventure needs a crew.

Buddy brings the enthusiasm. Malley brings the plan.

Oscar mostly wonders why either of them is doing this in the first place.

Buddy

The Enthusiast

  • Energetic
  • Curious
  • Playful
  • Loyal

Buddy is always ready for the next adventure — preferably right now.

Whether he's exploring somewhere new, chasing something interesting, or getting completely carried away with the latest plan, Buddy approaches life with enthusiasm and absolutely no shortage of energy.

His philosophy is simple:

“Why think about it when we could already be doing it?”

Malley

The Planner

  • Smart
  • Confident
  • Determined
  • Caring

Malley is usually the one thinking things through.

She's clever, confident, and more likely to have a plan before charging into the unknown — although being around Buddy means those plans don't always survive first contact with the adventure.

Her philosophy:

“We can absolutely do this. But maybe we should have a plan.”

Oscar

The Observer

  • Dry
  • Unimpressed
  • Observant
  • Patient-ish

Oscar is the black-and-white cat who has somehow found himself living in the middle of Buddy and Malley's chaos.

While Buddy is charging ahead and Malley is figuring out what they should actually be doing, Oscar prefers a comfortable spot on the couch where he can monitor the situation from a safe distance.

He rarely joins the chaos.

He does, however, judge it.

Constantly.

His philosophy:

“Seriously?”

Better Together

Buddy brings the enthusiasm.

Malley brings the plan.

Oscar is just tired of them both.

Somehow, it works.

Together, Buddy and Malley are always ready for the next adventure.

Oscar would prefer they have their adventures somewhere else.

Our World

Where everyday life becomes an adventure

Buddy & Malley Play Co. started with two real Australian Shepherds, Buddy and Malley, and a simple idea: what if the adventures dogs imagine were every bit as big as they seem to them?

A ball isn't always just a ball. A walk isn't necessarily just a walk. And sometimes the next adventure might lead all the way to the Moon.

Buddy and Malley's world takes the personalities, curiosity, chaos and companionship of the real dogs and turns them into stories where almost anything can happen.

Inspired by the Real Buddy & Malley

Behind every adventure are the real Buddy and Malley.

Their personalities, habits and relationship inspire the characters you see in our stories. Buddy's enthusiasm and curiosity have a habit of getting things moving, while Malley's thoughtful nature helps keep the team on course.

The stories may be imaginary. The dogs behind them aren't.

Big Adventures. Small Moments.

Not every Buddy & Malley story needs a rocket.

Their world can be a campsite in the woods, an afternoon outside, a mysterious new discovery or something interesting sitting at the bottom of the toy box.

Some adventures are enormous. Others begin with the kinds of ordinary moments every dog owner recognizes.

That's part of the fun.

The Adventure Keeps Growing

Buddy and Malley's world isn't built around one story.

Each adventure becomes another chapter—from their earliest escapades to lunar expeditions, camping trips and whatever they manage to get themselves into next.

Along the way, you'll also find shorts, behind-the-scenes looks at how their adventures are created, and things from the real Buddy and Malley's lives.

Because around here, you never really know where the next adventure will begin.

Two Aussies. One big world. Endless possibilities.
