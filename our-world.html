Our World

Explore the world behind the adventures.

Every Buddy & Malley adventure introduces new places, vehicles, equipment, and a few familiar things they never want too far away.

Take a closer look at the objects and locations that have become part of their world.

Lunar Expedition

Built to Go Beyond Earth

Getting two Australian Shepherds to the Moon takes a little more than enthusiasm and a good plan.

From the spacecraft that carried them there to the equipment that kept them safe along the way, these are some of the vehicles, structures, and technology behind Buddy and Malley's Lunar Expedition.

Space Exploration Agency

Lunar Expedition

The starting point for Buddy and Malley's journey to the Moon.

The Space Exploration Agency's Lunar Mission Complex is home to the launch facilities, mission operations, and technology needed to send Buddy and Malley beyond Earth.

Rocket

Lunar Expedition

Buddy and Malley's ride to space.

Built for their Lunar Expedition, the rocket carried them from the launch pad and began their journey toward the Moon.

LEM

Lunar Expedition

Their spacecraft for exploring the Moon.

The Lunar Excursion Module gave Buddy and Malley a way to travel to and operate on the lunar surface during their mission.

Rocket Cradle

Lunar Expedition

Even astronauts need a good seat.

Buddy and Malley's specially designed rocket cradle keeps them secure and comfortable while traveling aboard the spacecraft.

Spacesuit

Lunar Expedition

Designed for adventures where ordinary fur isn't quite enough.

Buddy and Malley's spacesuits provide the protection they need while working and exploring beyond Earth.

Space Helmet

Lunar Expedition

An essential part of their spacesuits and one of the most recognizable pieces of their Lunar Expedition equipment.

Transport Ring

Lunar Expedition

A specialized piece of mission equipment used to safely transport moon samples collected during the mission.
